About This Website

Welcome to our Mathematics Learning Website. This website was developed to support learners in understanding Mensuration, with particular emphasis on the area of triangles and quadrilaterals.

Our Purpose

The purpose of this website is to make the learning of mathematics more engaging, interactive and accessible. Learners can study lesson content, explore interactive activities, practise mathematical problems and complete an online assessment.

What We Offer

  • Mathematics lesson content
  • Interactive learning activities
  • Practice exercises
  • Online assessment
  • Technology-supported mathematics learning

Our Goal

Our goal is to help learners develop confidence in solving problems involving the area of triangles and quadrilaterals while encouraging the use of technology in mathematics learning.
