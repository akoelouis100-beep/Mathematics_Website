What is Interactive Learning?

Interactive learning allows learners to explore mathematical concepts by actively working with digital tools. In this lesson, GeoGebra and Desmos will be used to investigate the relationship between the dimensions of shapes and their areas.

Learners should experiment with the dimensions of the shapes, observe what changes and explain what they discover.

1. GeoGebra Activity

Use GeoGebra to explore the area of a triangle. Change the base and height and observe how the area changes.

Triangle Area Investigation

Formula: A = ½ × b × h

Activity:

  1. Change the base of the triangle.
  2. Change the perpendicular height.
  3. Observe the change in area.
  4. Explain what happens when the base is doubled.

Open GeoGebra

2. Desmos Graph Activity

Desmos can be used to investigate mathematical relationships visually. Use the Desmos graphing calculator to explore relationships between dimensions and area.

Rectangle Area Investigation

Consider the relationship:

A = length × width

  1. Open the Desmos calculator.
  2. Enter mathematical expressions.
  3. Change the values of the dimensions.
  4. Observe how the area changes.
  5. Describe the relationship you observe.

Open Desmos

3. Video Learning

Watch an instructional video on the area of triangles and quadrilaterals. While watching, pay attention to the formulas and worked examples.

4. Interactive Challenge

A rectangular classroom has a length of 12 m and a width of 8 m.

Challenge: What is the area of the classroom?

Use the formula:

A = length × width

Try solving the problem before checking your answer.

A = 12 × 8

A = 96 m²

Reflection

After completing the activities, answer the following questions:

  1. What did you observe when the base of the triangle increased?
  2. What happened to the area when the height increased?
  3. How did the digital tools help you understand area?
  4. Which activity did you find most useful? Explain why.

Digital Citizenship

When using online mathematics resources, learners should use them responsibly and ethically.

  • Give credit to the creators of online resources.
  • Respect copyright and intellectual property.
  • Do not copy and submit another person's work as your own.
  • Use AI responsibly as a learning support.
  • Verify information obtained from online sources.
  • Do not share private information online.
