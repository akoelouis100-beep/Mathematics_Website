Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners should be able to:

  • Explain the meaning of area.
  • Identify different types of triangles and quadrilaterals.
  • State the formulas for finding areas.
  • Calculate the area of different shapes.
  • Apply area formulas to real-life problems.
  • Use digital tools to explore geometric shapes.
