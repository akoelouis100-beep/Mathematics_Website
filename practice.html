Practice Instructions

Read each question carefully and try to solve it before checking the worked examples or hints.

Remember to include the correct square unit in your final answer.

Question 1: Area of a Triangle

A triangle has a base of 12 cm and a perpendicular height of 8 cm. Find its area.

Hint

Use the formula:

A = ½ × base × height

View Worked Solution

A = ½ × 12 × 8

A = 48

Answer = 48 cm²

Question 2: Area of a Rectangle

A rectangular classroom has a length of 15 m and a width of 6 m. Find the area of the classroom.

Hint

Remember:

A = length × width

View Worked Solution

A = 15 × 6

A = 90

Answer = 90 m²

Question 3: Area of a Square

A square has a side length of 9 cm. Find its area.

Hint

Use:

A =s²

View Worked Solution

A = 9 × 9

A = 81

Answer = 81 cm²

Question 4: Area of a Parallelogram

A parallelogram has a base of 14 cm and a perpendicular height of 7 cm. Find its area.

Hint

Use:

A = base × perpendicular height

View Worked Solution

A = 14 × 7

A = 98

Answer = 98 cm²

Question 5: Area of a Trapezium

A trapezium has parallel sides of 10 cm and 16 cm and a perpendicular height of 6 cm. Find its area.

Hint

Use:

A = ½×(a + b)×h

View Worked Solution

A = ½×(10 + 16)× 6

A = ½ × 26 × 6

A = 78

Answer = 78 cm²

Challenge Question

A triangular piece of land has a base of 20 m and a perpendicular height of 15 m. A rectangular piece of land measuring 10 m by 8 m is added to it.

Find the total area of the two pieces of land.

View Solution

Area of triangle:

½× 20 × 15 = 150 m²

Area of rectangle:

10 × 8 = 80 m²

Total area:

150 + 80 = 230 m²
